## Adjusted versus raw reconciliation

With f = Adj Close / Close, a distribution D (dividend plus capital gain) with ex-date t should give f(t-1) / f(t) = 1 - D / Close(t-1), and f should not move on other days. `total_gap` compares the log of the whole adjustment with the sum of the log distribution factors; `max_unexplained_step` is the largest move of f on a day without a distribution. Splits do not enter f, because Yahoo's Close is already split-adjusted (see the corporate-action dates). The annual gap is the adjusted minus the raw annualised return; the implied distribution yield is the average annual sum of D / Close(t-1).
